\section{Model C: Superhuman Autonomous Multi-Agent Adaptation}
\label{sec:model_c}

While Model B established dominant performance against stationary heuristic archetypes (+120.76 BB/100, 28.0\% freezeout tournament championship rate), empirical analysis against human experts revealed three fundamental vulnerabilities:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Absence of Adversarial Sparring:} Model B trained exclusively against rule-based bots and had never faced an adaptive neural opponent seeking to exploit its own frequencies.
    \item \textbf{Intra-Hand Action Amnesia:} While the 63-dimensional state vector captured aggregate pot and bet totals, it lacked visibility into \textit{how} the chips arrived in the pot, remaining vulnerable to deceptive check-raise traps and multi-card draw bluffs.
    \item \textbf{Static Prior Inertia:} The unweighted Beta-Binomial conjugate tracker required 20--30 hands to register behavioral shifts, failing to capitalize on rapid human psychological tilt.
\end{enumerate}

To overcome these constraints, we developed \textbf{Model C (Superhuman Counter-Adversary)}, integrating three algorithmic innovations: (1) Multi-Agent League Sparring via Fictitious Play, (2) an expanded 87-dimensional Intra-Hand Action Sequence Vector, and (3) a Dual-Timescale Recency Bayesian Profiler with exponential decay discounting.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Multi-Agent League Sparring (Fictitious Play)}
\label{subsec:league}

To prevent policy collapse and ensure robust defense against counter-exploitative strategies, Model C replaces stationary bot tables with a dynamic \textbf{League Pool} $\mathcal{P}$. The 5-seat table environment samples opponents according to a balanced 50/50 mixture:
\begin{equation}
    P(\text{Seat } s \sim \text{Neural League}) = 0.50, \quad P(\text{Seat } s \sim \text{Heuristic}) = 0.50
\end{equation}
The neural opponent pool is initialized with the frozen production weights of Model B:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{P}_0 = \{\pi_{\text{Model B}}\}
\end{equation}
During the 1,500,000-timestep training trajectory, rolling policy snapshots are saved every 250,000 steps and immediately injected into the active sparring pool:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{P}_k = \mathcal{P}_{k-1} \cup \{\pi^{(k \times 250\text{k})}\}, \quad k \in \{1, 2, \dots, 6\}
\end{equation}
By the conclusion of training, the agent sparred simultaneously against 7 distinct historical iterations of itself and Model B. This eliminates bluffing loopholes and forces the emergence of robust 3-bet defense and positional control.

\subsection{Intra-Hand Sequence Memory (87-Dimensional Observation)}
\label{subsec:sequence_obs}

To maintain compatibility with \texttt{MaskablePPO} while avoiding recurrent LSTM stability overhead, Model C explicitly concatenates intra-hand betting trajectories into an expanded \textbf{87-dimensional scale-invariant observation vector} $\mathbf{s} \in [-1.0, 1.0]^{87}$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{s} = \left[ \mathbf{s}_{\text{base}}^{(63)} \;\|\; \mathbf{h}_{\text{pre}}^{(8)} \;\|\; \mathbf{h}_{\text{post}}^{(8)} \;\|\; \mathbf{h}_{\text{line}}^{(8)} \right]
\end{equation}
where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\mathbf{s}_{\text{base}}^{(63)}$ encodes private card features, hand strength, pot odds, stack ratios, phase one-hot vectors, position, table survival, and lifetime Bayesian opponent profiles.
    \item $\mathbf{h}_{\text{pre}}^{(8)}$ encodes pre-draw action types (normalized from $-1.0$ for fold to $+1.0$ for all-in) and normalized committed chips $\frac{b_{\text{pre}}}{C_{\text{table}}}$ across all 4 relative opponent seats.
    \item $\mathbf{h}_{\text{post}}^{(8)}$ encodes post-draw action types and normalized post-draw commitment $\frac{b_{\text{post}}}{C_{\text{table}}}$.
    \item $\mathbf{h}_{\text{line}}^{(8)}$ encodes categorical deceptive line combinations (check-raise trap: $+1.0$, pat value bet: $+0.8$, multi-card draw bluff: $-0.5$) paired with dynamic tilt deltas.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Dual-Timescale Recency Bayesian Profiler}
\label{subsec:recency}

To detect sudden emotional tilt or aggressive gear shifts, each table seat tracks two conjugate Beta-Binomial models in parallel:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Lifetime Macro Prior:} $\text{Beta}(\alpha_0, \beta_0)$ with prior weight $w_0 = 5.0$, accumulating unweighted historical actions to characterize core player archetype.
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Micro Recency Tracker:} Implements exponential forgetting with discount factor $\lambda = 0.90$ (half-life $t_{1/2} \approx 6.6$ hands, effective horizon $H = \frac{1}{1-\lambda} = 10.0$ hands):
    \begin{align}
        \tilde{N}_t &= \lambda \cdot \tilde{N}_{t-1} + 1.0 \\
        \tilde{K}_t &= \lambda \cdot \tilde{K}_{t-1} + k_t, \quad k_t \in \{0, 1\}
    \end{align}
    yielding the dynamic posterior expectation:
    \begin{equation}
        \mathbb{E}[\theta_{\text{micro}}] = \frac{\alpha_{\mu} \cdot w_{\mu} + \tilde{K}_t}{w_{\mu} + \tilde{N}_t}
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}
The policy receives the composite behavioral shift delta:
\begin{equation}
    \Delta_{\text{tilt}} = (\mu_{\text{micro}} - \mu_{\text{macro}})_{\text{VPIP}} + (\mu_{\text{micro}} - \mu_{\text{macro}})_{\text{PFR}} + (\mu_{\text{micro}} - \mu_{\text{macro}})_{\text{AF}}
\end{equation}
When an opponent tilts ($\Delta_{\text{tilt}} > 0.25$), the alert triggers within 3 hands, instructing the policy to widen its value-isolation range and punish sub-optimal aggression.
